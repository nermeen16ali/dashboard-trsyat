Description

Al Rajhi Company seeks through this tender to update its technical infrastructure by supplying and installing a complete set of desktop and laptop computers. The process aims to meet the needs of various departments, improve employee efficiency, and ensure device compatibility with the latest systems and applications used within the company. The scope of work also includes equipping the devices with all necessary components and basic software for operation.
The tender also includes the winning supplier's commitment to providing periodic maintenance and Support for two years, including addressing emergency faults, providing original spare parts, and submitting periodic performance reports to the technical administration. The supplier is also expected to adhere to a specific delivery and installation schedule, while training the internal team on optimal device usage, to ensure the sustainability of technical solutions and enhance the company's operational efficiency.
