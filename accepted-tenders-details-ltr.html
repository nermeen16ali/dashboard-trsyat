Description

Al-Rajhi Company seeks through this tender to modernize its technical infrastructure by supplying and installing a comprehensive set of desktop and laptop computers. The process aims to meet the needs of various departments, improve employee efficiency, and ensure device compatibility with the latest systems and applications used within the company. The scope of work also includes equipping the devices with all necessary basic components and software for operation.
The tender also includes the winning supplier's commitment to provide periodic maintenance and technical support for two years, including addressing emergency breakdowns, providing original spare parts, and submitting periodic performance reports to the technical management. The supplier is also expected to adhere to a specific schedule for delivery and installation, with training for the internal team on the optimal use of the devices to ensure the sustainability of technical solutions and enhance the company's operational efficiency.
